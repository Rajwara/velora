Overview

Your car's service indicator knows when it needs attention. We follow the exact manufacturer schedule, fit OEM-quality parts and record everything in your digital service history.

German manufacturers use condition-based service intervals that change with how and where you drive. We read your car's service data, follow the matching procedure step by step and only replace what the schedule or our inspection says is due.

Every service ends with a road test, a reset of the service indicator and a written report with photos, so you always know what was done and what's coming next.

Questions

Servicing Questions.

More answers on our FAQ page.

Will an independent service affect my warranty?

No. Under EU rules you can have your car serviced by an independent specialist without losing the manufacturer warranty, as long as the manufacturer's specifications and parts of matching quality are used.

Do you update the digital service history?

Yes. Every service is recorded so your history stays complete for warranty claims and resale.

Which oil do you use?

Only oils carrying the approval your manufacturer specifies for your engine, such as the relevant BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Porsche or VW approval.

Can I wait while you work?

For an oil service, yes. Our lounge in Schwabing has coffee and Wi-Fi. For longer jobs we offer pick-up and delivery.
