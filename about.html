Our Story

Velora started with a simple idea: the people who rent you a Porsche should be the same people who know how to look after one.

Founded in 2014 by Kfz-Meister Markus Brandt, Velora began as a small independent workshop for German performance cars. Owners came for manufacturer-spec servicing without dealer prices and stayed for the honest advice.

Today we combine a curated rental fleet with a full care studio under one roof in Munich. Because every rental car is maintained by our own technicians, we hand over cars we know inside out.

Milestones

From Two Bays to Forty Cars.

  1. 2014

    Velora opens a two-bay workshop in Schwabing, specialising in BMW and Porsche servicing.

  2. 2017

    The detailing studio opens, adding paint correction and ceramic coating.

  3. 2020

    Our rental fleet launches with six cars, all maintained in-house.

  4. 2023

    Testing partner on site for HU/AU, plus climate-controlled tyre storage.

  5. 2026

    40+ performance cars in the fleet and over 3,500 services completed.
