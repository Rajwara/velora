Tyre Service & Storage

The Right Tyres for Every Season

Seasonal wheel changes, balancing, alignment and climate-controlled storage for German performance cars up to 23 inches.

Overview

Germany's winter tyre rule is situational: in wintry conditions your car must be on tyres with the alpine symbol. We switch your sets, store the other one and remind you when it's time.

Performance cars often run staggered sizes, run-flat tyres and tyre pressure sensors. We fit and balance them with equipment designed for large, low-profile wheels, and torque every wheel bolt to specification.

Your stored set is washed, checked for tread depth and age, and kept in a climate-controlled room until next season.

Questions

Tyre Service Questions.

More answers on our FAQ page.

When should I fit winter tyres?

The law is situational, but many drivers follow the 'O to O' rule: from October to Easter (Ostern). In southern Bavaria and the Alps, earlier is better.

What tread depth do I need?

The legal minimum is 1.6 mm. For winter tyres we recommend at least 4 mm.

Do you sell tyres?

Yes. We source approved tyres for your model, including manufacturer-marked tyres such as Porsche N-rated or BMW star-marked.

Do you handle tyre pressure sensors?

Yes. We check, program and replace TPMS sensors where needed.
