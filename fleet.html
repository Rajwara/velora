Rental Requirements

Simple Rules, No Surprises.

Minimum Age 25

Drivers must be at least 25. Some high-performance models require 30+.

Licence for 3+ Years

A valid licence held for at least three years. Non-EU licences need an International Driving Permit.

Credit Card Deposit

A deposit from €2,500 is pre-authorised on a credit card in the main driver's name.

Fully Insured

Comprehensive insurance included, with optional excess reduction at checkout.
