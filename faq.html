Rental

How old do I need to be to rent?

At least 25, with a driving licence held for three years or more. For some high-performance models such as the G 63 or GT 63 the minimum age is 30.

Can I use a non-EU driving licence?

Yes. If your licence was issued outside the EU/EEA, please bring an International Driving Permit or an official translation together with your passport.

How much is the deposit?

From €2,500, depending on the car. It is pre-authorised on a credit card in the main driver's name and released after the car is returned in good condition. Debit cards are not accepted for the deposit.

How many kilometres are included?

250 km per day. Extra kilometre packages can be booked in advance at a lower rate than the standard per-kilometre charge.

Can I drive to other countries?

Trips to Austria, Switzerland, Italy, France and the Benelux countries are possible on request. Remember vignettes for Austrian and Swiss motorways.

Do you deliver cars?

Yes, to hotels and addresses in Munich and to Munich Airport (MUC). Delivery charges depend on location and time.

Care & Service

Will servicing with you affect my manufacturer warranty?

No. Under EU rules you can have your car serviced by an independent specialist without losing the warranty, as long as manufacturer specifications and parts of matching quality are used. We document every service in your digital service history.

Which brands do you work on?

Porsche, BMW and MINI, Mercedes-Benz and Mercedes-AMG, Audi, Volkswagen, Cupra and other VW Group brands.

Can you do the HU/AU (TÜV)?

Yes. We prepare your car and our on-site testing partner carries out the general inspection and emissions test.

Do you offer pick-up for servicing?

Yes, across Munich. We collect your car, service it and bring it back, usually the same day.

How long does a ceramic coating take?

Usually two to three days including paint correction and curing time.

Payment

Are prices shown including VAT?

Yes. All prices are in euros and include 19% German VAT (MwSt.).

Which payment methods do you accept?

Credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), EC/Girocard and bank transfer for services. Rental deposits require a credit card.

What is your cancellation policy?

Rentals can be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before pick-up. Service appointments can be moved or cancelled free of charge up to 24 hours before.
