Road Trip from Munich

Lake Garda via Brenner

See the Route Reserve the G 63
The Drive

Munich to Lake Garda is a classic long weekend. You cross the Alps at the Brenner Pass and arrive at the north end of the lake in around four and a half hours.

The fastest route runs via Innsbruck and the Brenner motorway into South Tyrol, then down the Adige valley to Riva del Garda. Once there, the Gardesana roads along both shores are the reward.

A G 63 offers space for luggage and a commanding view for the long run south. For the return, consider the slower route over the Reschen Pass for something different.

At a Glance

  • Time3–4 days
